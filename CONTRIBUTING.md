# Research discussions

This archive is for reading and critical exchange. The [Issues](../../issues) page contains a dedicated thread for each publicly listed paper.

A useful contribution identifies the **paper and passage**, gives the **objection or proposed test**, and explains what finding would change the conclusion. Corrections to citation metadata, broken links, and verified reproduction failures are equally welcome.

Please do not submit private records, credentials, unpublished manuscripts, or confidential material. Avoid treating a preprint as a peer-reviewed finding. Respect the license and citation terms of each original work.
