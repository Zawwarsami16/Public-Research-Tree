# The Person Before the Split

*Soul–Body Unity, Personal Identity, and the Living Human Being*

**Zawwar Sami** · Public preprint (not a peer-reviewed journal article)

This philosophical inquiry asks whether personal identity should be understood first through the living, embodied person rather than treating mental and bodily continuity as separable starting points.

[**Full paper (PDF)**](Zawwar-Sami-The-Person-Before-the-Split.pdf) · [Official page](https://zawwarsami.com/writing/the-person-before-the-split) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/23127396) · [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/SAMTPB)

**For discussion:** If memories and personality traits were held constant in a hypothetical case, which dimensions of bodily life would still matter to personal identity, and why?

[**Open the paper discussion**](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/issues/9) — objections, competing interpretations, and close readings welcome.
