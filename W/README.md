## Book I — current pathway

W1 establishes why component performance does not by itself demonstrate sustained capability. W2 examines what development evidence can establish and which next observation addresses the remaining uncertainty. Further studies will investigate scientific constraints, materials, energy, mobility and land, air and maritime platforms before developing an original standalone synthesis. Later Book II and III research remains part of the longer programme, not a published milestone.
