# W — Weapons, Warfare & Defence

**Military technology, autonomous and strategic systems, defence, and the human consequences of conflict.**

My [Long-Term Vision](https://zawwarsami.com/research#W) follows a three-book research plan. The currently visible pathway focuses on **Book I — Weapons Science & Technology**, with twelve planned studies and a future standalone synthesis book. **W1 is the first released paper**; W2–W12 remain planned.

## Book I — current pathway

W1 establishes why component performance does not by itself demonstrate sustained capability. Further studies will investigate scientific constraints, materials, energy, mobility and land, air and maritime platforms before developing an original standalone synthesis. Later Book II and III research remains part of the longer programme, not a published milestone.
