# W2 — What Development Evidence Can and Cannot Show

*AAG, EMALS and model-dependent engineering tests*  
**Zawwar Sami** · W Series · **Independent preprint v1.2** · 17 October 2026 · 19 pages

A successful demonstration does not establish dependable service. I examine documented AAG aircraft-recovery and EMALS launch-system milestones, then use four worked analyses to investigate peak loads, reliability confidence, mechanism identification and changes in configuration.

A test contrast can separate explanations in an additive model while leaving time and interaction effects indistinguishable in a larger model family. Exact calculations also show how assumed count variability changes the repetitions needed for a specified decision. These are analytical examples and synthetic computations, not measured carrier performance or a claim to new statistical theory.
