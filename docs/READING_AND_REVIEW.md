# Reading and reviewing

This archive is public research, not a finished consensus. The original paper record on Zenodo, PhilPapers or the author's website identifies the edition. A GitHub PDF here is a versioned reading copy.

## Pick one thread

- **Agent evaluation:** [A8 — From Knowing to Doing](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/A/A8) · [discussion #8](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/issues/8). Focus on the difference between a tool being available, an invocation being attempted, and a verified change in the outside world.
- **Memory and continuity:** [A5 — Persistent State and Becoming](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/A/A5) · [discussion #5](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/issues/5). Look for an event-order or supersession case in which identical stored sentences lead to different correct next actions.
- **Personhood:** [The Person Before the Split](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/P/The-Person-Before-the-Split) · [discussion #9](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/issues/9). Examine the premise that a person is first a living, embodied unity.

## Make an objection useful

Open the discussion linked from the paper's folder. A brief comment is sufficient if it names:

1. **The claim or passage** you are responding to (page, section or short description).
2. **A counterexample, rival interpretation, missing reference, or test** that could put pressure on the claim.
3. **What would follow** if your objection holds, including a limitation or alternative outcome.

A negative result is as useful as a positive one. Reproducible test cases, corrected citations, and clear questions are welcome. There is no need to endorse the paper or to present a full review.

Please do not post API credentials, private records or unpublished sources. Do not present a preprint as peer-reviewed research.
