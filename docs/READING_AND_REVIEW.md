# Reading and reviewing

This archive is public research, not a finished consensus. The original paper record on Zenodo, PhilPapers or the author's website identifies the edition. A GitHub PDF here is a versioned reading copy.

## Choose the right place to contribute

- **GitHub Discussions:** [Join the community board](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/discussions). The pinned welcome post explains the conversation; focused **Q&A** topics on A8, A5 and W1 invite independent answers, counterexamples and reproducible tests.
- **Paper-specific issues:** Use the issue linked in each Q&A to identify exact passages, attach corrections and follow evidence related to a single manuscript.

## Pick one research question

- **Agent evaluation:** [A8 — From Knowing to Doing](../A/A8/) · [paper issue #8](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/issues/8). What verifies an actual external state change rather than a tool invocation or an agent's self-report?
- **Memory and continuity:** [A5 — Persistent State and Becoming](../A/A5/) · [paper issue #5](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/issues/5). What event-order or supersession case would cause identical stored records to require different next actions?
- **Operational capability:** [W1 — Weapons as Systems](../W/W1/) · [paper issue #11](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/issues/11). Can separate component availability figures establish jointly usable service without dependence information?
- **Personhood:** [The Person Before the Split](../P/The-Person-Before-the-Split/) · [paper issue #9](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/issues/9). Examine the premise that a person is first a living, embodied unity.

## Make an objection useful

Open the relevant [GitHub Q&A](https://github.com/Zawwarsami16/Public-Research-Tree/discussions) to debate the question, or comment on a specific paper issue to document an exact correction. A brief contribution is sufficient if it names:

1. **The claim or passage** you are responding to (page, section or short description).
2. **A counterexample, rival interpretation, missing reference, or test** that could put pressure on the claim.
3. **What would follow** if your objection holds, including a limitation or alternative outcome.

A negative result is as useful as a positive one. Reproducible test cases, corrected citations, and clear questions are welcome. There is no need to endorse the paper or to present a full review.

Please do not post API credentials, private records or unpublished sources. Do not present a preprint as peer-reviewed research.
